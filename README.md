#Data-Driven Sales Analysis: Coffee Business Case Study
________________________________________


Executive Summery

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of coffee sales data spanning approximately three years across three markets: the United States, Ireland, and the United Kingdom. 

The analysis covers 1,000 orders across four coffee types (Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, and Robusta), three roast profiles, and four pack sizes.
 
 Total revenue over the period amounts to $45,134, providing valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and market distribution.

________________________________________
Business Problems Identified:
1. Market Concentration Risk
   
The United States generates $35,639 (79%) of total revenue, while Ireland ($6,697) and the United Kingdom ($2,799) contribute significantly less.

This heavy reliance on one market creates a high business risk.

3. Volatile Demand
   
Sales trends show strong fluctuations over time with no consistent growth pattern across all coffee types.
This instability makes forecasting, inventory planning, and resource allocation difficult.

4. Underutilized Top Product (Light Roast)
   
Light roast leads with $17,354, outperforming Medium ($14,600) and Dark ($13,179).
Despite this, there is no clear strategy focusing on promoting the top-performing product.

6. Ineffective Loyalty Program
   
Customers without a loyalty card generate more revenue ($24,216) than loyalty members ($20,918).
This suggests the loyalty program is not delivering its intended value.

Recommendations

•	Reduce market dependency by expanding efforts in Ireland and the UK 

•	Stabilize sales through better forecasting and targeted promotions 

•	Focus on Light Roast as the top-performing product 

•	Improve the loyalty program to increase customer retention and spending 

•	Leverage customer insights for better targeting and personalization 

These recommendations aim to improve revenue diversification, enhance customer retention, and support more stable and scalable business growth.
